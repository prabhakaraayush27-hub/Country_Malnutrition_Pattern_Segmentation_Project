The Model Pipeline & Mathematical Process

How raw World Bank & UNICEF indicators are mathematically transformed into actionable public health clusters.

1. Z-Score Standardization (StandardScaler)

Since child stunting ranges from 2% to 55% while wasting ranges from 0.5% to 20%, unscaled Euclidean distances would be heavily biased by higher-magnitude variables. Every indicator is centered to mean 0 and unit variance:

z_j = (x_j - μ_j) / σ_j,    for j ∈ {1, ..., 9}

2. Partition Clustering & Centroid Assignment

The model iteratively partitions 166 sovereign states into K disjoint clusters C = {C_1, ..., C_K} to minimize the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS / Inertia):

WCSS = ∑_{k=1}^K ∑_{i ∈ C_k} || z_i - c_k ||_2^2

Inference Assignment Rule: Any query profile z is assigned to cluster k_hat = argmin_k d(z, c_k).

3. Dimensionality Reduction (Principal Component Analysis)

Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) of covariance matrix Σ = (1/n) Z^T Z extracts orthogonal eigenvectors V. The top two components explain 65.8% of total global variance:

  • PC1 (48.5% Variance): Undernutrition axis (heavy positive loadings on stunting, wasting, anaemia; negative on obesity).
  • PC2 (17.3% Variance): Acute wasting vs. chronic stunting and exclusive breastfeeding.

4. Model Validation: Why K = 3 Was Selected

Evaluated via Silhouette score, Davies-Bouldin index, and 20-round bootstrap stability (ARI across 80% subsamples):
